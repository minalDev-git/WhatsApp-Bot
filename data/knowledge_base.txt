UNIVERSITY FYP & EVENTS - KNOWLEDGE BASE

ABOUT FINAL YEAR PROJECT (FYP)
The Final Year Project is a capstone requirement for all undergraduate students. It's an opportunity to apply theoretical knowledge to real-world problems. Students work individually or in teams (2-4 members) under faculty supervision to develop innovative solutions. FYP is worth 6 credit hours and spans both Fall and Spring semesters.

FYP TIMELINE & DEADLINES
- Project Proposal Submission: August 15, 2026
- Supervisor Allocation: August 30, 2026
- Mid-Semester Evaluation: October 10, 2026
- Progress Report Submission: November 20, 2026
- Final Presentation & Defense: January 5-10, 2027
- Final Report Submission: January 15, 2027

FYP PROCESS STAGES
1. Ideation: Brainstorm project ideas aligned with your interests
2. Proposal Writing: Document problem statement, objectives, methodology
3. Literature Review: Research existing solutions and related work
4. Development: Build and implement your solution
5. Testing & Evaluation: Validate your work with testing
6. Documentation: Write comprehensive final report
7. Presentation: Present findings to evaluation committee

FYP PROJECT IDEAS - AI & AGENTS
1. Chatbot Development: Build intelligent conversational agents using NLP
2. Recommendation System: Create AI-powered content recommendation engine
3. Autonomous Agents: Develop multi-agent systems for task coordination
4. Computer Vision: Object detection, image classification, or segmentation
5. Natural Language Processing: Text analysis, sentiment analysis, or translation
6. Machine Learning Pipeline: Predictive modeling for real-world datasets
7. Reinforcement Learning: Train agents to optimize decision-making
8. Generative AI: Build text/image generation models
9. Voice Assistant: Create voice-enabled AI assistant
10. Anomaly Detection: Detect fraudulent transactions or network intrusions

AI & AGENTS - EMERGING AREAS
- Large Language Models (LLMs): Fine-tuning and prompt engineering
- Retrieval Augmented Generation (RAG): Combining LLMs with knowledge bases
- Multi-Agent Systems: Agents collaborating to solve complex problems
- AI Ethics: Bias detection, explainability, fairness in AI
- Knowledge Graphs: Structured data representation for intelligent systems
- Computer Vision + NLP: Multimodal AI applications

FYP EVALUATION CRITERIA
- Problem Significance: Relevance and impact of the problem
- Technical Complexity: Difficulty and sophistication of solution
- Innovation: Novelty and creativity of approach
- Implementation Quality: Code quality, documentation, testing
- Presentation: Clear communication of work and results
- Report Quality: Comprehensive documentation and analysis
- Defense Performance: Ability to answer questions and defend work

GRADING BREAKDOWN
- Proposal & Supervisor Assessment: 10%
- Progress Reports & Meetings: 20%
- Final Project Implementation: 40%
- Final Report: 20%
- Presentation & Defense: 10%
